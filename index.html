Hi, I’m Grace.

  • I recently moved from NYC to LA.
  • I work as a Sr Data Scientist at Uniswap. Crypto went from a fascination to a career & I’ve been lucky enough to have had various jobs in the industry for the past 5 years.
  • I find joy in taking film photographs, developing & printing them.
  • My writing comes in two forms: an outlet for my imagination, or a way to learn what I don’t know. I am working on sharing both of these more publicly.
  • I’ve recently become obsessed with materials. It grew out of a fascination around how difficult it is to remove plastics from my life (yes the microplastics frenzy got to me) and became a deeper love for learning how raw materials are transformed & the supply chains that distribute them across the globe. If you’re also curious, I’ve put together a list of resources I’ve been consuming to learn!

    The main resources I’ve been reading or listening to in order to learn about the use cases & supply chains of raw materials.

    Books

    • Material World Ed Conway
    • The Material Advantage Bernard J. Bulkin
    • Stuff Matters Mark Miodownik
    • How the World Really Works Vaclav Smil
    • Trade World Ed Conway
    • Elements BBC World Service, presented by Justin Rowlatt
    • Making the Modern World Vaclav Smil

    Datasets

    • Historical import/export of materials to the US USGS

    Podcasts

    • Materialism hosted by two professors of materials science, Taylor Sparks & Andrew Falkowski

    Blogs & news

    • Construction Physics sometimes covers materials, like this piece on helium
    • The Commodity Strategist Substack
    • The Oregon Group Substack
    • Reuters commodities section
